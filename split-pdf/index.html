How to split a PDF

  1. Choose your PDF.Select the document you want to split or extract pages from.
  2. Enter page numbers.Use commas for separate pages and hyphens for ranges, for example 1-3, 6, 9-12.
  3. Extract and download.The selected pages are copied into one new PDF file.
Privacy: page extraction runs in your browser. This version accepts PDFs up to 100 MB.

Extract selected pages from a PDF

Use the free SoloBizKit Split PDF tool when you only need part of a larger document. Select a single page, several separate pages or complete page ranges and save those pages as a new PDF.

Split PDF by page ranges

You can enter ranges such as 1-5 or combine ranges and individual pages such as 1-3, 7, 10-12. The selected pages are placed into the new PDF in page-number order.

Useful for reports, contracts and scanned documents

Page extraction can help when a report contains only a few pages you need to share, a contract has an appendix you want separately, or a scanned document includes pages that belong in another file.
