What should an invoice include?

  • Your business or seller information.
  • The customer's relevant billing information.
  • A clear invoice number and issue date.
  • A description of products or services, quantities and prices.
  • Applicable taxes, total amount due and payment terms.

Invoice requirements can vary by country, tax status and transaction type. Check the rules that apply to your business before relying on a template.
