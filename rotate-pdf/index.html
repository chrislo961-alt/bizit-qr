How to rotate a PDF

  1. Choose your PDF.Select the document with pages facing the wrong direction.
  2. Pick an angle.Rotate all pages 90° clockwise, 180° or 90° counter-clockwise.
  3. Download the corrected PDF.The page content is preserved while the page rotation is updated.

Fix sideways or upside-down PDF pages

Use the free SoloBizKit Rotate PDF tool when scanned pages, exported documents or downloaded PDFs have the wrong orientation. Rotate the complete document and save a new corrected copy.

Rotate PDF pages 90, 180 or 270 degrees

Choose 90 degrees clockwise for pages turned left, 180 degrees for upside-down pages, or 90 degrees counter-clockwise for pages turned right. The selected rotation is applied across the PDF.

Rotate a PDF without converting the document

This tool updates the PDF page rotation instead of turning pages into images. That helps preserve the existing page content and document structure while correcting the orientation.

Free PDF rotation without an account

The current Rotate PDF tool is free to use and does not require a SoloBizKit account. Password-protected or damaged PDFs may need to be unlocked or repaired before the browser can process them.
