## Analytics

SoloBizKit uses Google Analytics only after a visitor chooses to accept analytics cookies.
Analytics is used to understand aggregate usage such as page visits and interactions with tools.
SoloBizKit's analytics implementation does not intentionally send values entered into calculators,
QR-code fields, invoice fields, or business-name fields as analytics event parameters.

Visitors can decline analytics cookies. If consent is declined, the analytics script is not loaded.
