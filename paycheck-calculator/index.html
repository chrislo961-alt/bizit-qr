How this 2026 paycheck estimate works

The calculator converts salary or hourly wages into pay-period gross income, subtracts your entered deductions and withholding estimates, and applies 2026 employee Social Security and Medicare payroll-tax rates. Social Security is limited by the 2026 annual wage base.

What is the Social Security tax rate in 2026?

The employee Social Security tax rate is 6.2% on covered wages up to the 2026 wage base of $184,500.

What is the Medicare tax rate in 2026?

The standard employee Medicare tax rate is 1.45%. Certain higher earners may also be subject to Additional Medicare Tax, which this simplified estimator does not automatically calculate.

Why do I enter my own federal and state withholding percentages?

Actual income-tax withholding is personal and can depend on filing status, W-4 elections, credits, deductions, multiple jobs and state/local rules. Adjustable rates make this version transparent instead of pretending one generic percentage is exact for everyone.
