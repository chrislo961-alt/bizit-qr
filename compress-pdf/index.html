Important limitation

Browser-only PDF compression cannot reliably shrink every PDF. Files that are already optimized, or whose size comes mainly from compressed photos, may not get meaningfully smaller without re-rendering pages and reducing image quality.

We won't fake the result. If the optimized copy is not smaller, SoloBizKit tells you and keeps the original rather than claiming a percentage that was not achieved.

Reduce PDF size when possible

This first version performs safe structural optimization. It can help some documents, while already-optimized or image-heavy PDFs may need lossy image recompression for a large reduction. We prefer an honest result over silently damaging document quality.
