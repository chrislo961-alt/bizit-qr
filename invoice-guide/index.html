A simple workflow

  1. Use a unique invoice number so invoices are easy to track.
  2. Clearly identify the seller, client, work performed and amount due.
  3. State payment terms and the due date plainly.
  4. Keep your own copy of every invoice for your records.
Good to know: SoloBizKit tools are designed for practical estimates and simple workflows. Requirements for taxes, invoices, registrations and business records can vary by location, so check the rules that apply to you.
