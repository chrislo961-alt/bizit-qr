Privacy Policy

A plain-language overview of how SoloBizKit handles data.

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Information you enter into tools

SoloBizKit is designed so that many tool operations can happen locally in your browser. We do not require an account to use the core tools currently available.

Local storage

Some features may use your browser's local storage to remember preferences or saved items on your device. Clearing browser data may remove those saved items.

Analytics and hosting

Our hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs as part of delivering and securing the website. Before analytics or advertising services are added, this policy should be updated to describe them accurately.

Sensitive information

Do not enter confidential, highly sensitive or regulated information into SoloBizKit tools unless a tool explicitly states that it is designed to handle such data.

Changes

We may update this policy as the service changes. The date above indicates the latest revision.

Analytics

SoloBizKit uses Google Analytics only after you choose to accept analytics cookies. We use analytics to understand aggregate usage such as page visits and interactions with tools.

Our analytics implementation is designed not to send values entered into calculator fields, QR-code fields, invoice fields or business-name fields as analytics event parameters.

If you decline analytics cookies, Google Analytics is not loaded. Your consent choice is stored locally in your browser.

Advertising

SoloBizKit may use Google AdSense to display advertising. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, subject to the visitor's choices and applicable consent requirements.

Advertising settings and consent requirements may vary by region. SoloBizKit does not intentionally send the contents of files or values entered into tools to Google AdSense as ad-targeting data.
