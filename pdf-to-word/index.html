You can add multiple PDF files in one upload. Choose whether you want one Word document per PDF or a single combined Word document.

What is converted?

This browser-based converter extracts selectable text from each PDF page and creates an editable Word document. Page breaks are preserved between PDF pages.

Important: PDF and Word are very different formats. Complex layouts, scanned pages, forms, tables, columns and embedded graphics may not reproduce exactly. Image-only scanned PDFs need OCR, which this version does not perform.

Privacy

The conversion is designed to happen in your browser. SoloBizKit does not intentionally upload your document contents to a SoloBizKit server.
