How to create a QR code with a logo

A logo can make a QR code feel more connected to your brand. Create the QR destination first, then open the full SoloBizKit QR Code Generator, upload a square logo, choose strong error correction and export the finished code as PNG or SVG.

Why add a logo to a QR code?

Logo QR codes can make printed materials feel more intentional and can help people recognize the business or organization behind the code. They are commonly used on packaging, signs, menus, event material, posters and business cards.

How large should the logo be?

The logo should stay relatively small because it covers part of the QR pattern. The full SoloBizKit generator places the logo in the center and uses strong error correction by default when a logo is added. Even then, always scan-test the final export before printing.

Tips for a reliable branded QR code

  • Use a simple square logo with clear edges.
  • Keep strong contrast between the QR pattern and background.
  • Leave a clean white area around the QR code.
  • Avoid covering too much of the QR pattern with the logo.
  • Use strong error correction when adding a logo.
  • Scan-test the final PNG or SVG on multiple phones before printing large quantities.

QR code with logo FAQ

Can I make a QR code with my logo for free?

Yes. The current full SoloBizKit QR generator supports logo upload and does not require a SoloBizKit account.

Does the logo have to be square?

No, but a simple square or near-square logo usually fits more naturally in the center of a QR code.

Can a logo make a QR code unreadable?

Yes, if it is too large or the QR code has poor contrast. Keep the logo small, use strong error correction and test the code before publishing it.

Can I download a logo QR code as SVG?

The full SoloBizKit QR generator offers PNG and SVG download options. If you use a logo, scan-test the actual exported result before using it in print.
